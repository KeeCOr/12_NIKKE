## IARC Questionnaire Answers

Use the following answers when completing the IARC questionnaire at [https://www.globalratings.com](https://www.globalratings.com).

### Violence

| Question | Answer | Notes |
|----------|--------|-------|
| Does the game contain violence? | Yes — mild | Squads attack a boss; stylized damage numbers and hit effects. No realistic injury depiction. |
| Is violence graphic or gory? | No | No blood, dismemberment, or realistic injury. |
| Is violence directed at real-world identifiable groups? | No | Combat is against fictional monster bosses. |
| Does the player control the violence? | Partial | Combat is automated (auto-battle). The player configures squads; they do not aim weapons directly. |

---

## Developer Notes

- Auto-battle means the player never directly aims or fires; combat is resolved algorithmically.
- Boss weak points are presented as colored body-part indicators, not as realistic anatomy.
- No characters die in a graphic or realistic manner.
- The game contains no monetization layer of any kind.
