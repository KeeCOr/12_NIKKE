# Privacy Policy — SquadVsMonster

**Last updated:** 2026-09-05

---

## Overview

SquadVsMonster is a singleplayer, offline PC game built with Unity and distributed on Steam. This policy explains what data the game does and does not collect.

---

## Data We Do Not Collect

SquadVsMonster does **not** collect, transmit, or store any of the following:

- Personal identification information (name, email address, date of birth)
- IP addresses or device identifiers
- Location data
- Payment or financial information
- User-generated content uploaded to external servers
- Gameplay telemetry or analytics sent to our servers
- Chat or communication data

---

## Local Data Storage

The game saves the following data **locally on your device only**:

| Data | Purpose | Location |
|------|---------|----------|
| Game settings (resolution, volume) | Restore preferences on launch | Local application data folder |
| Save state (squad configuration, progress) | Continue from where you left off | Local application data folder |

This data never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time by removing the game's save folder.

---

## Steam Platform Data

SquadVsMonster is distributed through Steam (Valve Corporation). Steam may collect platform-level data such as playtime, achievements, and crash reports according to the [Steam Privacy Policy](https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/). We have no control over and no access to data collected by Steam.

---

## Third-Party Services

SquadVsMonster does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising networks, or social SDKs.

---

## Children's Privacy

SquadVsMonster does not collect any data from any user, including children under the age of 13. The game has no online features, no account creation, and no communication channels.

---

## Changes to This Policy

If the game is updated to include online features in the future, this policy will be updated before those features are released. The updated policy will be posted on the Steam store page and within the game.

---

## Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the developer through the Steam Community hub for SquadVsMonster.
