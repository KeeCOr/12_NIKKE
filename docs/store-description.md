## Full Description

**SquadVsMonster** is a session-based boss-rush auto-battler where assembling the right squad is only the beginning. Before each wave, you review your squad's composition preview — is your build firepower-heavy, defense-thin, or range-mixed? Then the battle runs itself, but the HUD keeps you informed: boss weak points, barrier pressure, reload states, and squad role breakdowns all update in real time.

When the fight ends, a one-line result cue tells you exactly where the run fell apart — survival collapse, firepower shortage, reload overlap, or a missed finishing window. Take that information, adjust your squad, and go again.

**Core loop:**
- Set your squad's roles and check the composition preview
- Watch the auto-battle while tracking boss weak points and reload timing
- Read the post-battle result summary and its cue line
- Adjust your lineup and retry

**Key features:**
- Squad composition preview before each fight — know your build's strengths before the first shot
- Live CombatAdvisor HUD during battle — boss parts, barrier threat, reload count, and down count
- Post-battle result summaries with one-line cue: survival failure, firepower failure, reload overlap, or timing failure
- Multiple boss body parts (CORE / CHEST / LEG / ARM) with distinct weak-point priorities
- Fast session design — most runs complete in under 30 seconds, perfect for quick play

**Who is this for?**
If you enjoy auto-battle RPGs or mobile boss-rush games and want to understand *why* your comp won or lost rather than just watching numbers fly, SquadVsMonster is built for that moment of clarity.
